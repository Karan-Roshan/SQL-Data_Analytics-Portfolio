Pizza Hut SQL Case Study – Key Business Findings

Basic Analysis
1. Total Orders Placed
- Determined the total number of customer orders processed during the analysis period to understand overall business activity.

2. Total Revenue Generated
- Calculated the total revenue earned from pizza sales, providing an overview of the company's overall sales performance.

3. Highest-Priced Pizza
- Identified the premium-priced pizza available on the menu to understand the highest-value product offering.

4. Most Common Pizza Size Ordered
- Analyzed customer purchasing behavior to determine the most frequently ordered pizza size.

5. Top 5 Most Ordered Pizza Types
- Identified the five best-selling pizza types based on total order quantity to highlight customer favorites.


Intermediate Analysis
6. Category-wise Quantity Ordered
- Calculated the total quantity sold for each pizza category (Classic, Chicken, Supreme, Veggie) to identify the most popular product category.

7. Order Distribution by Hour
- Examined order patterns throughout the day to identify peak business hours and customer purchasing trends.

8. Category-wise Pizza Distribution
- Analyzed the distribution of pizza offerings across different categories to understand the product portfolio.

9. Average Pizzas Ordered Per Day
- Grouped orders by date and calculated the average daily pizza sales to evaluate daily business performance.

10. Top 3 Pizza Types by Revenue
- Identified the three highest revenue-generating pizza types to understand which products contribute most to sales.


Advanced Analysis
11. Revenue Contribution by Pizza Type
- Calculated the percentage contribution of each pizza type to total revenue to identify the most profitable products.

12. Cumulative Revenue Analysis
- Performed cumulative revenue analysis over time to visualize overall business growth and revenue trends.

13. Top 3 Revenue-Generating Pizzas Within Each Category
- Ranked the top three pizza types by revenue within each category, enabling comparison of best-performing products across categories.


Overall Business Insights
- Identified overall sales performance through total orders and revenue.
- Discovered customer preferences by analyzing pizza sizes, categories, and best-selling pizza types.
- Determined peak ordering hours to support workforce planning and operational efficiency.
- Evaluated category-wise sales performance for better inventory and menu management.
- Identified the highest revenue-generating pizzas to support pricing and marketing strategies.
- Measured each pizza type's contribution to overall revenue to prioritize high-performing products.
- Analyzed cumulative revenue trends to monitor business growth over time.
